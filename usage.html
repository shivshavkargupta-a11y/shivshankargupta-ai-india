Usage Terms

The rules for using our services — in plain words.

1. How it works

The brain of our service lives on the server. Your phone only sends the question and shows the answer. So an internet connection is required — without it, no answer will come.

2. Voice

Voice also comes from the internet. No internet means no voice.

3. Your chats

Your chats are kept safely on the server. You can delete them whenever you wish — once deleted, they will not come back.

4. What not to do

  • Do not try to break or hack our services
  • Do not use them to deceive others
  • Do not ask for anything that breaks the law or harms anyone
  • Do not tell anyone your password

5. About the truth of answers

Answers are created by AI. Sometimes an answer may be wrong. For anything important — such as medicine, money or the law — please check for yourself and ask someone qualified.

6. Deleting your account

You may delete your account at any time. When you do, all your chats and information are gone for good.
